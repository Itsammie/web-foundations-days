About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight tool designed to help you capture your thoughts instantly. It provides a fast, distraction-free environment to organize your daily tasks and ideas before they slip away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the input field on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save your thought.
  3. Review your saved notes in the list provided.

Features

  • Instant note creation
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Easy to navigate dashboard
